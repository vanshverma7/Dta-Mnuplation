To swap your driving test with a friend, follow these steps:
Ensure both of you have a valid driving test booking.
Call the DVSA on 0300 200 1122 and provide each other's booking reference numbers.
Confirm the swap with the DVSA agent.
Both of you will receive confirmation via email from the DVSA with the new test dates.
This process is completely legal and does not involve any payment or exchange of money. It is important to note that both parties must be available to speak with the DVSA
